## Unit 1 Our school

**单词**：school 学校 / classroom 教室 / music room 音乐教室 / art room 美术教室 / science room 科学教室 / library 图书馆

**对话**
- Hello! Welcome to our school. 你好！欢迎来到我们的学校。
- Hi! Your school is beautiful. 嗨！你的学校真漂亮。
- Thank you! Look! This is our library. 谢谢！看！这是我们的图书馆。
- Wow, it's big! 哇，它好大！

**Chant**
> School day, happy day.
> Library, read all day.
> Music room, sing and play.
> Art room, draw today.
> Science room, fun we play.
> Classroom, learn the happy way.

## Unit 3 Our weekdays

**单词**：Monday 星期一 / Tuesday 星期二 / Wednesday 星期三 / Thursday 星期四 / Friday 星期五

**活动**：jump rope 跳绳 / go swimming 去游泳 / play ping-pong 打乒乓球 / go skating 去滑冰 / play football 踢足球

**对话**
- It's Friday. Let's play football! 今天是星期五。我们踢足球吧！
- OK! Let's play together! 好呀！我们一起玩吧！

**Chant**
> Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday. / Thursday and Friday.
> Weekdays, weekdays, weekdays! / Fine days, fine days!

**Project / Share**：课后体育活动表 —— It's Monday. Let's play football.

## Unit 5 Our weekends

**单词**：clean my room 打扫我的房间 / go to the museum 去博物馆 / make New Year cards 做新年贺卡 / sing and dance 唱歌跳舞 / visit grandma and grandpa 看望爷爷奶奶

**对话**
- What will you do this weekend? 这个周末你打算做什么？
- I will visit my grandma and grandpa on Saturday. 星期六我要去看望爷爷奶奶。
- I will make New Year cards on Sunday. 星期天我要做新年贺卡。

**Chant**
> Weekends! Weekends! What will you do?
> Clean my room! Cool! Cool! Cool!
> Sing and dance! Good! Good! Good!
> Go to the museum! Go! Go! Go!
> Make New Year cards! Fun! Fun! Fun!
> Visit grandma, grandpa too! Hooray!

**Project / Share**：My weekend plan —— Hello! My name is Li Jinjin. This is my weekend plan. I will ... on Saturday. ...
